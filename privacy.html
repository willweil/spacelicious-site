Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This policy describes how Spacelicious (“we,” “us”) handles information when you use our website at spacelicious.com and our mobile apps, including SockNinja.

Summary

Our apps are built to work on your device. SockNinja does not collect any personal data: it has no account, no uploads, no ads, and no tracking, and its photos and scan history never leave your iPhone.

SockNinja

  • Camera and photos. SockNinja asks for camera access, and optionally photo library access, so it can find socks in the picture you point it at or choose. Sock detection runs entirely on your iPhone. Images are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • Scan history. Past scans and pairs are saved only in the app’s storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • No accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking. SockNinja does not ask you to sign in, does not include advertising or third-party analytics, and does not track you across apps or websites.
  • App Store privacy details. SockNinja’s App Store listing states “Data Not Collected.” See its App Store page.

Information you send us

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and help you, and we delete support emails when they are no longer needed. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time by emailing hello@spacelicious.com.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising, and it has no sign-in. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record standard technical information such as IP addresses in server logs for security and operations; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. When you download an app through the Apple App Store, Apple’s privacy policy also applies to that transaction.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Children’s privacy

SockNinja is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children. Our website and support channel are not directed to children under 13; if you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can turn off camera or photo access for SockNinja at any time in your iPhone’s Settings. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, which in practice is limited to support emails; contact us to make a request.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
